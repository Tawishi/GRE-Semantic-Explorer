Welcome to the GRE Semantic Explorer. This application analyzes high-yield vocabulary from the Magoosh 1000 list. Rather than alphabetical memorization, this tool groups words logically to help you master Sentence Equivalence (finding identical sentence fillers) and Text Completion (understanding nuanced pivots and contrasts).

Antonym Pairs & Contrasts

Crucial for Text Completion. Look for pivot words (e.g., "however", "although", "rather than") which require you to find the antonym of a concept established earlier in the sentence.

Tricky Words & False Friends

The GRE loves to test words that sound like they mean one thing, but actually mean something entirely different (or the exact opposite). Master these to avoid common traps.
